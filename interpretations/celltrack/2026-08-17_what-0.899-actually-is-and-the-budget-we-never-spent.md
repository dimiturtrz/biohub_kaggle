# What 0.899 actually is, and the budget we never spent

*2026-08-17 — comparing every from-scratch attempt against the run that scored 0.899,
and the contradiction that comparison exposes*

## What the 0.899 pipeline is

The 0.899 LB submission is **pilkwang weights + flow + fusion + symmetry-division**,
linked by a global `AssignmentLinker`
(`2026-08-12_dense-mislinks-are-a-wall.md:8`, `2026-08-11_the-term-we-never-contested.md:11`).
Two submissions scored 0.899 *identically* at different fork budgets (cap 300 / ~1072
forks vs cap 100 / ~400 forks) — the score is not fork-count sensitive
(`2026-08-11_the-term-we-never-contested.md:100`). The underlying weights are the mounted
pilkwang weights; the linker/post-proc is ours.

The proxy→LB transfer law
(`2026-08-09_proxy-lb-transfer-law.md`) is the ruler: proxy moves the LB **only when it
changes the linking decision rule** — recall the sparse proxy already sees does not
transfer, and some reverse (smooth 0.8→0.3: +0.008 proxy, −0.003 LB). Its verdict: *"0.892
is an asset gap, not a tuning gap. Proxy-measurable optimization is exhausted."* The
warm-pipeline ceiling stands at LB 0.892–0.899 / proxy 0.847.

## The contradiction the comparison exposes

**I have been calling the lever "synthetic pretraining." The frontier used no
pretraining.** `2026-08-16_from-scratch-ceiling-resolved-lever-is-pretraining.md:4`
corrects itself in its own body: *"the frontier uses no pretraining — it is supervised
joint training for 50 epochs on all 199 movies."* l.86: *"its edge is supervised training
budget (50 epochs × all199)."*

So the pilkwang weights I warm-start from were **built by a recipe I have never run**:
50 epochs of joint supervised training on all 199 movies. My from-scratch chain
(`scratch_v1`) is a **2-epoch** cosine run. That is a ~25× epoch-budget gap before any
question of movies-seen.

This reframes the confusor thesis
(`2026-08-17_the-confusor-the-loss-never-saw.md`). That doc argues the wall is
*annotation completeness* — sparse real labels can't supply the unannotated confusors, so
a synthetic complete-candidate corpus is needed. But the 0.899 weights reached the ceiling
on the **same sparse annotation**, by spending **budget and data volume** instead. If
50 epochs × all199 on real sparse labels reaches 0.899, then:

- annotation-completeness is **not** the wall — training budget is;
- the synthetic corpus is at best a *shortcut* to a budget I could spend directly;
- the "representation defect / inv~0.99" verdict is a candidate **undertraining
  artifact** — a faulty-TRAINING harness suspect (CLAUDE.md) wearing a representation
  verdict's clothes.

**Caveat that keeps this a question, not a verdict (train is underannotated — ~1–2% of
cells per frame carry a label, `core/data/tracks.py:157` `annotated_fraction =
labelled_nodes / estimated_node_count`; 133318 labelled nodes / 199 videos;
`tests/e2e/dataset_facts.py`).** The "pilkwang = 50 epochs × all199 on the SAME sparse
labels, no pretraining" recipe is **this repo's own inference, not a cited primary
pilkwang source** — flagged unverified. So I do NOT actually know pilkwang trained on the
same underannotated data; it could have used denser labels, external data, or a pretrained
backbone, none of which I can replicate. Treating that inference as fact is itself a
chain-of-assumptions error.

**Correction (2026-08-17, from the owner): raw budget is not the lever — recipe quality
is.** Most of the loss falls in the first epochs; a well-made recipe (diversity + good
loss) accelerates convergence, so pilkwang's 0.847 should be reachable from scratch in
**~10 epochs, not 50**. 50 is pilkwang's brute-force inefficiency, not a bar to clear. This
demotes the "budget arm" from a 20-epoch grind to a **saturation probe**: plain from-scratch
will flatten early (epoch ~2–3), and that flattening height is only the *no-recipe floor*.
It does NOT distinguish "annotation wall" from "plain recipe saturated" — the earlier
either/or was wrong. Only a good-recipe arm separates them.

**Plain probe result (2026-08-17, killed at epoch 1 — question answered).** Two evals:

| epoch | proxy | node R | edge loss | sanity AUC | inv | P_chosen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 0.6777 | 0.798 | 0.0802 | 0.9985 | 1.00 | 0.354 |
| 1.0 | 0.6726 | 0.883 | 0.0040 | 1.0000 | 1.00 | **0.542** |

Proxy FLAT (−0.005, noise). Edge loss collapsed 0.080→0.004 and sanity AUC→1.0 — the head
fits the training corpus **perfectly** — yet inv stayed PINNED at 1.00 and P_chosen *rose*
(0.354→0.542, more confidently wrong). This is the 2.6%-contest scarcity live: more budget
on the uncontested corpus overfits the easy choice, it does **not** fix linking. Budget is
refuted as the linking lever; the confusor thesis is confirmed. node R converged to the same
~0.883 from-scratch detection ceiling as scratch_v1 (won't break 0.906 plain). Killed at
epoch 1 and the GPU repurposed to the good-recipe arms — the plain probe had no more signal
worth 7h. **This is the recipe>budget prediction landing exactly.**

The two arms, cost-tiered:

1. **plain saturation probe** (running): from-scratch, no recipe tricks. Read epochs
   0.5/1/2/3; once it flattens, KILL it (don't grind 20 plain epochs). Gives the no-recipe
   floor + the saturation shape.
2. **good-recipe contender** (decisive): diversity (synthetic complete-candidates raising
   in-gate contest 2.6%→~100% + oversample the 10×-denser 6bba acquisition) + contest-focused
   loss + prior-velocity, ~10 epochs. If it climbs **higher and faster** than the plain floor
   and approaches 0.847 → recipe is the lever, pilkwang reachable from scratch, and this is the
   elegant fast-converging solution. If it too stalls at the plain floor → the ~1–2% annotation
   is a genuine ceiling on real data and synthetic supervision must carry more of the corpus.

Because pilkwang's data is unverified, the arms decide it **empirically, on my own curve**:

- from-scratch, real sparse labels, many epochs (walk toward 50), no synthetic, no
  pretraining. If proxy **climbs toward 0.847** → budget is the lever on sparse data →
  synthetic demoted to a shortcut. If it **re-plateaus at ~0.70 under full lr budget** →
  the ~1–2% annotation is the wall (pilkwang then had something I can't match) → synthetic,
  which supplies a **fully-annotated** complete-candidate corpus, is the from-scratch-legal
  way to break it. A re-plateau does NOT refute synthetic — it CONFIRMS the annotation wall.

**Third lever the density fact exposes:** the two acquisitions differ ~10× in annotation
density (`dataset_facts.py:5`; 44b6 = 71 videos, 6bba = 128 videos). Oversampling the
denser acquisition puts more true confusors per batch **within** the sparse constraint —
a data lever that costs no synthetic machinery. Untested.

scratch_v1 also confounds budget with schedule: `--epochs 2 --cosine-lr` decays lr to ~0
by step 36k, so its 0.697 plateau is partly a **schedule artifact** (lr dead before the
edge representation forms), not purely a budget ceiling. The budget arm runs cosine over
the full horizon to remove that confound.

## Impact ranking (the plan was sequenced by ease, not impact)

The 0.15 proxy gap (from-scratch ~0.70 → warm 0.847) splits by mechanism, ranked by
plausible share:

1. **Detection recall — ~0.08, HALF the gap, most under-attacked.** From-scratch 0.906 vs
   warm 0.986. Whether 0.906 is a *recipe-limited* or *hard* from-scratch ceiling is the
   open question — same recipe>budget logic as the edge plateau. The running plain arm
   probes it FOR FREE (det loss on, 20 epochs): watch node R past 0.906 (recipe-limited →
   big lever, run a focal/positive-weight detection-recall recipe) vs stalled (hard ceiling
   → 0.08 unrecoverable from scratch).
2. **Edge/linking contest — ~0.07 remainder.** The confusor/2.6%-contest work. Real but the
   smaller half.
3. Recipe efficiency (loss/schedule) — multiplier on 1&2, not standalone.
4. Density-oversample, analytical linker motion re-tune — second-order, bounded.

**Consequence for reading the arms: track TWO saturation curves, not one** — node R
(detection, #1 lever) AND proxy/inv (linking, #2). Earlier I watched only proxy/inv, which
blinds the bigger lever.

## Second, smaller contradiction: "detection is not the cap"

My memory (`celltrack-rift-is-objective-not-target`) states *"detection is NOT the cap
(node R 0.882 near ceiling 0.906); linking is."* But 0.906 is from-scratch's **own**
ceiling — warm reaches **0.986** (`..gap-is-detection-generalization.md:22`). "Near
ceiling 0.906" undersells an 0.08 detection-recall gap that is part of the deficit. The
cap is **both** detection generalization *and* edge representation, and both trace to the
same unspent budget. Memory corrected.
