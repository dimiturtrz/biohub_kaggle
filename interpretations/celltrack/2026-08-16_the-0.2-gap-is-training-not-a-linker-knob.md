# The 0.2-to-max gap is detection + affinity training, not a linker knob — measured, not asserted

*2026-08-16 — locates where the ~0.2 points from our ~0.895 to the 1.1 ceiling actually live, by decomposing
both score terms on the CV-8 proxy and refuting the one free in-architecture lever (boundary re-pricing) with
a five-arm sweep. Companion to `2026-08-16_from-scratch-ceiling-resolved-lever-is-pretraining.md`
(which settled that the frontier's edge is supervised training budget, not SSL).*

## The denominator: where the 0.2 splits

Score = `adjusted_edge_jaccard + 0.1 * division_jaccard`, max 1.1. CV-8 proxy, shipped recipe (flow linker,
thr 0.97, divisions on):

| movie | adj edge jac | node ratio | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 44b6_0b24845f | 1.04→1.0 | −0.40 | fine (raw jac 1.0; estimate high) |
| 6bba_05b6850b | 0.981 | −0.05 | fine |
| 44b6_0113de3b | 0.963 | −0.03 | fine |
| 6bba_969618f6 | 0.941 | +0.13 | over-detects |
| 44b6_341df25f | 0.941 | +0.12 | over-detects |
| 6bba_05db0fb1 (dense) | **0.903** | +0.00 | detection fine → **pure linking residual** |
| 6bba_fc83837d | **0.837** | +0.06 | linking + some detection |
| 44b6_e57ff5c6 | **0.833** | **−0.19** | **under-detects 19%** |

Two facts fix the gap:

1. **The division term is ≈0 and largely unearnable.** Division true-positives are `0` on *every* movie
   (`div = 0/fp/fn` throughout) → `division_jaccard ≈ 0`, so the 0.1-weighted term contributes ~0. It is not
   a linker knob: a fork needs the *second daughter detected*, and the second daughter is usually undetected
   (a detection limit). Shipped divisions bought only +0.004 on the LB. The 0.1 weight is mostly a
   detection-gated ceiling, not free headroom.

2. **The edge gap is carried by two movies, and their cause is detection.** The mean is dragged down by
   `e57ff5c6` (0.833, under-detecting 19% — missing cells take their edges with them) and `fc83837d`
   (0.837). The best movies already sit at 0.96–1.0. `e57` is detection-limited by construction; even the
   dense movie's *raw* residual (below) is an affinity problem, not a boundary one.

## The dense residual is affinity-inverted mislinks, not mispriced boundaries

Dense movie (`6bba_05db0fb1`, the worst-linking, detection-clean movie) false-positive decomposition
(`dense_diagnosis --charges`): jaccard 0.903, **TP 1132, FP 71, FN 51**.

| FP charge | count | share | mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| MISLINK | 37 | 52% | wrong successor, **97.3% affinity-inverted** (mean P_true 0.079 vs P_chosen 0.581) |
| TARGET_ORPHAN | 33 | 46% | over-link past a true track end ("stole a lost target") |
| EXTRA_CHILD | 1 | 1% | — |

Both dominant charges are the edge head ranking the wrong next-frame cell high. That is an **affinity-quality**
failure — the head's `P(s→t)` is inverted on the crowded pairs — not a linker decision the operating point can
re-make.

## The one free in-architecture lever (boundary re-pricing) is refuted

TARGET_ORPHAN is a glue-link: `source-disappear + target-appear` replaced by one spurious continuation. The
flow linker prices exactly this — `boundary_cost` (shipped `disappearance_cost=3.0`, symmetric) and the built
but untested `appearance_cost` asymmetry (price a birth ≠ a death; mid-movie births are common, deaths rarer).
If over-linking were a boundary-economics problem, cheaper births would let the target start fresh instead of
receiving the glue-link. **It does not.** CV-8 sweep:

| arm | proxy score | Δ vs baseline |
|---|---|---|
| baseline (symmetric 3.0) | ~0.910 | — |
| appearance_cost=6.0 | 0.9101 | +0.0002 |
| appearance_cost=1.5 | 0.9078 | −0.0021 |
| disappearance_cost=1.5 | 0.9051 | −0.0048 |
| appearance_cost=0.5 | 0.9050 | −0.0049 |

The whole surface is flat-to-negative within ±0.005 — the shipped `3.0` sits at the optimum, and cheaper
boundaries *monotonically hurt*. **Re-pricing a track boundary cannot fix an affinity-inverted mislink**: the
glue-links are chosen because the head scores the wrong successor above the right one, not because ending a
track is overpriced. This matches the charge decomposition (97.3% affinity-inverted → "retrain target").

## Conclusion: no ≥0.02 in-architecture lever; the gap is training

Both decompositions converge on the same two training levers, and every no-train knob measured is under the
0.02 floor the campaign does not fight below 0.95:

- **Affinity quality** (the 52% affinity-inverted mislinks on crowded frames) — a better-trained edge head.
- **Detection budget** (the 19%-under-detecting movie; the undetected second daughters that zero the division
  term) — the parked 50-epoch × all-199 supervised-joint lever from the companion doc.

Candidate *non-training* levers that remain — top-k-to-estimated-count detection (to flatten the −0.40…+0.13
per-movie node ratios a single global threshold produces) or a per-movie threshold — are **threshold-class:
the sparse proxy is recall-blind there** (operating_point's standing note), so they can only be judged on the
leaderboard, and with one submission left they are a gamble, not a proxy-validated win. They are noted, not
taken, unprompted.

**The honest big target is training, not a decision rule.** The linker is already at its operating optimum on
the knobs the proxy can see.

## Reproduce

- Denominator: `python -m celltrack.eval.proxy_eval --config paths.yaml --device cuda --cv --per-movie`.
- FP decomposition: `python -m celltrack.eval.dense_diagnosis --config paths.yaml --device cuda --charges`.
- Boundary sweep: `proxy_eval --cv --set linker.appearance_cost=<v>` / `--set linker.disappearance_cost=<v>`
  (the four arms above). No code landed — all knobs already exist on `LinkerConfig`; this is measurement.
